# RDC01015: Constant not used

**Severity:** Information &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Family:** `RDC` (Rubberduck Core analyzers) &nbsp;·&nbsp;
**Raised by:** `ConstantNotUsedAnalyzer`

Nothing refers to a constant.

A constant that is never used does nothing for the program. It is often left over from code that changed, or a name typed where another was meant.

Only the constants of a procedure are counted. A statement of the procedure, the value of another of its constants and the bounds of one of its arrays all
use a constant. A constant of the module may be used by the declarations of the module or by another module, and is not reported.

The finding is the name of the constant.

## How it is raised

|Situation|Example|Resolution|
|---|---|---|
|A constant of a procedure that nothing refers to.|`Const Limit As Long = 10`|Remove it, or use it where it was meant to be used.|
