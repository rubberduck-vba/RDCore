# RDC11006 — Ambiguous concatenation

**Severity:** Information &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Family:** `RDC` (Rubberduck Core analyzers) &nbsp;·&nbsp;
**Raised by:** `AmbiguousConcatenationAnalyzer`

The `+` operator is given two `String` values, and joins them.

`+` adds numbers, and joins strings: which one it does depends on the types of its two operands ([**MS-VBAL §5.6.9.3** Arithmetic Operators](../specs/rd-vbal.5.6.9.3.arithmetic-operators.md)). A reader has to
know both types to know what the line does, and the same line does the other thing when one of the operands is changed to a number: `"1" + "2"` is `"12"`, but
`"1" + 2` is `3`. The `&` operator always joins, whatever it is given, and says so.

## How it is raised

|Situation|Example|Resolution|
|---|---|---|
|Two `String` values are joined with `+`.|`fullName = firstName + " " + lastName`|Use `&`: `fullName = firstName & " " & lastName`.|
