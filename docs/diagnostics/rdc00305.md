# RDC00305 — Obsolete Global modifier

**Severity:** Information &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Family:** `RDC` (Rubberduck Core analyzers) &nbsp;·&nbsp;
**Raised by:** [`ObsoleteGlobalModifierAnalyzer`](../api/RDCore.Diagnostics.Analyzers.ObsoleteGlobalModifierAnalyzer.html)

A variable or a constant is declared `Global`.

A `Global` declaration is a `Public` one ([**MS-VBAL §5.2.3** Module Declarations](../specs/rd-vbal.5.2.3.module-declarations.md)): the grammar accepts either keyword for a
variable and for a constant of a module, and they declare the same thing. `Public` is the keyword that is also used for procedures, types and enumerations, and the one the
language kept. The diagnostic is an opinion of the analyzer and not a condition of the language: both are legal VBA. It is reported for every language, though it may not mean the
same in all of them: `Global` is `Public` in VBA, and the analyzer makes no claim about VB6.

One finding is made for each statement, however many names it declares, and it is the keyword. It is a suggestion because `Public` does not change what the declaration is.

## How it is raised

|Situation|Example|Resolution|
|---|---|---|
|A module-level variable or constant is declared `Global`.|`Global Total As Long`|Declare it `Public`.|
